The Method

1. Never compare prices. Compare true monthly costs. A $150,000 “saving” spread over 30 years is a few hundred a month — a long commute can burn that before breakfast.

2. Miles are a bill, not a feeling. Every mile costs fuel, wear and a slice of the car’s life, whether you feel it that day or not.

3. Time is the silent tax. An extra hour a day is 250+ hours a year — more than six working weeks — spent unpaid, in traffic, for a house.

4. Breakeven tells the truth. Below, the app solves the far house’s breakeven price: what it would have to cost for the drive to actually be worth it.
